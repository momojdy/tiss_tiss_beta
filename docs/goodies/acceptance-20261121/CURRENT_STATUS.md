# Goodies acceptance — updated 22 November 2026, Asia/Manila

**Goodies is not yet production-ready, even with FAQ and translations excluded. All 12 phases have not passed their full acceptance requirements.** The tests below establish substantial working behavior; they do not certify every installed-screen interaction or an external provider/device integration.

Work continues on `main` in both repositories. The Wantiss application has not been released, submitted or updated over the air. No hosted Supabase test branch/project was created or restored. Financial tests used synthetic local accounts and balances plus real Stripe sandbox APIs. The payment repairs, private-proof hardening and USD/HTG producer were deployed under the owner's backend authorization. The new FX quote is configuration data; existing orders, balances, financial records and secrets were not changed. FAQ, translation and final policy/support approval remain deferred by the user. The owner also deferred leaked-password protection until a later Pro upgrade; it is no longer an active blocker in the present scope.
